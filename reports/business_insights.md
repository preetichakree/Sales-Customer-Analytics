# Retail Sales – Business Insights Report

## 1. Project Overview
This project analyses a synthetic retail sales dataset using MySQL, Python, Pandas and Matplotlib.

The objective is to understand sales performance, customer purchasing patterns and product category profitability.

## 2. Dataset Overview
- Total records: 20
- Total columns: 14
- Unique customers: 8
- Unique orders: 20
- Product categories: 3

## 3. Overall Business Performance

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | ₹2,46,040 |
| Total Profit | ₹55,640 |
| Total Orders | 20 |
| Total Customers | 8 |
| Profit Margin | 22.61% |

## 4. Category Performance

| Category | Revenue | Profit | Units Sold |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Electronics | ₹1,72,140 | ₹34,540 | 17 |
| Furniture | ₹64,400 | ₹16,900 | 7 |
| Stationery | ₹9,500 | ₹4,200 | 42 |

### Key Findings
- Electronics generated the highest revenue and profit.
- Furniture was the second-highest revenue contributor.
- Stationery sold the most units but generated the lowest revenue.

## 5. Monthly Sales Performance
January 2025 recorded the highest monthly revenue of ₹64,350.

## 6. Data Cleaning
- Checked for duplicate records.
- Handled missing values.

- Validated important sales fields.
